Privacy

Your details, in plain words.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect your contact details and what you plan in Know Your Home.
  • We use them to give you your report, improve the app, and contact you about your home and our services.
  • We do not sell your details.
  • You can ask us to remove them at any time.

Who we are

Know Your Home is built by Pocket Home, a Design + Build initiative based in Mogappair, Chennai. When this page says "we", it means Pocket Home.

What we collect

  • Your contact details: email address, mobile number and name.
  • Your plan: plot size, facing and locality, the number of floors, the spaces you choose, built-up areas, the package you pick and your cost range.
  • How you use the app: how far you got, how many times you tried, how many reports you made, the date and time, whether you used a phone or a laptop, and the link that brought you here.
  • Your feedback: your rating and anything you tell us we could do better.

Why we collect it

  • To give you your brief, your cost range and your report.
  • To understand how Know Your Home is being used, and to make it better.
  • To contact you about your home and about Pocket Home's services, by call, WhatsApp, message or email.
  • To tell you about Pocket Home, including through relevant messages or advertising on other websites and apps.

By giving us your details, you agree to this. You can change your mind whenever you like (see "Your choices" below).

Who sees it

Your details stay with the Pocket Home team. We do not sell them.

We rely on a few service providers to run the app. Your details are stored in our Google account, and the app is hosted on GitHub. If we run advertising, an advertising platform may process your contact details on our behalf so that we can reach you.

What stays on your device

Your browser remembers your email, mobile number and last plan, so that you can carry on next time without typing them again. If you use a shared device, choose "Use a different email" when you come back, or clear this site's data in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as they are useful for the reasons above, or until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

At any time, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, remove it, or stop contacting you. Just write or call, and we will take care of it.

  • Know Your Home is meant for adults planning a home. Please do not use it if you are under 18.
  • The costs in your report are indicative ranges based on market rates, not a quote. The Vaastu guidance is general. Your accurate cost comes once a design has taken shape.
  • If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
